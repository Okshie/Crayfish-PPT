CHAPTER 1BACKGROUND OF THE STUDY

Crayfish care needs
consistent attention.

01
≋

Water drives health

Growth and survival depend on clean water. Unstable temperature, pH, or quality causes stress and illness.

02
◷

Manual care is limited

Feeding, cleaning, and checking take time and manpower, and can lead to inconsistent care.

03
⌁

IoT can help

Sensors and a microcontroller give real-time updates and scheduled feeding, so changes are caught early.

Study aimHelp small-scale farmers monitor more reliably and cut routine manual workload.

CHAPTER 1SCOPE & DELIMITATIONS

Focused on small-scale
growing environments.

SCOPE

What the prototype covers

  • Monitors water temperature, pH, turbidity, and level
  • Scheduled feeding with preset quantities
  • Online monitoring and notifications
  • For small-scale and backyard growers
  • Tested in controlled or semi-controlled settings
DELIMITATIONS

What it does not cover

  • Other biochemical water parameters
  • Adaptive feeding (size, age, growth)
  • Breeding and harvesting
  • Disease diagnosis or treatment
  • Large commercial farms
  • Needs stable internet, power, and calibration

CHAPTER 3PROBLEM SPECIFICATION

Four problems in
small-scale farming.

01

Slow manual monitoring

Checking temperature, pH, and turbidity by hand delays detection of changes.

02

Inconsistent feeding

Forgotten, excessive, or insufficient feed causes waste, cost, and poor growth.

03

Waste buildup

No continuous monitoring or filtration lets waste and uneaten feed accumulate.

04

Costly tools

Existing systems can be too expensive or complex for small farmers.

NeedAn affordable IoT system for water monitoring, filtration, and scheduled feeding.
CHAPTER 3ANALYZE THE PROBLEM

From root cause
to design response.

CauseEffectDesign response
Feeding by estimate→Over- or underfeeding; waste raises pollution→Scheduled servo feeder, set quantity
No continuous monitoring→Late detection of temp, pH, turbidity shifts→Four sensors + live dashboard and alerts
Weak filtration→Waste and uneaten feed accumulate→Filtration pump circulates water
Limited automation access→Hard to manage water and feeding→Low-cost ESP32-based prototype
